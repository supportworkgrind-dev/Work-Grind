Access and security

Manage roles, invitations, and workspace members

Understand role eligibility, invitees, and who can change workspace access.

All workspaces

Symptoms

  • An invitee cannot join or a role is unavailable.
  • A member cannot open a page or sees fewer actions than expected.

Likely causes

  • Roles are restricted by workspace type and authority to assign them.
  • Pending invitation limits, plan limits, or expired links can block an invitation.

Steps to try

  1. 1Confirm the active workspace and its type.
  2. 2Ask an owner or authorized admin to open the member/invitation controls.
  3. 3Select a role eligible for that workspace: education roles are not interchangeable with company roles, and Dean / HOD are University-specific.
  4. 4Confirm the invitee email and ask them to accept using that address.
  5. 5If the link expired or the invitation is missing, ask the administrator to issue a new invitation after checking the plan’s pending-invitation limit.
  6. 6Never share an invitation link publicly; it may grant access to the workspace.

Verify the fix

Review the member list and confirm the accepted member has the intended role and workspace.

When to contact support

Contact the workspace owner/admin for role changes. Contact support for a blocked or misrouted invitation, including workspace type and error text.
