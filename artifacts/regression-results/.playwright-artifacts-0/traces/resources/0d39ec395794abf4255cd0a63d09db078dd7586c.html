Accounts and setup

Create an account and set up your workspace

Choose a workspace type, verify your email, and get through initial setup.

All workspaces

Symptoms

  • You cannot complete signup or do not know which workspace type to choose.
  • The verification message has not arrived, or setup appears incomplete.

Likely causes

  • The selected workspace type determines the available tools and role model.
  • Email delivery can take time or be filtered by your mail provider.

Steps to try

  1. 1Open Sign up and select Solo, Company, School / Academy, College, or University based on how the workspace will be used.
  2. 2Enter the requested account details and submit the form.
  3. 3Open the verification message sent to the address you entered and follow its instructions. Check spam or quarantine if it is missing.
  4. 4Sign in and complete the workspace setup shown for your selected type. The creator should have the ownership role for the new workspace.
  5. 5If you joined an existing workspace, use its invitation rather than creating a duplicate workspace.

Verify the fix

Sign in and confirm that the workspace name and type are correct. Its available navigation depends on that type, your role, and plan.

When to contact support

If verification still fails, use the public Contact page. Include the signup email and approximate time, but never send a password or verification code.
