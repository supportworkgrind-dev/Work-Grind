Set up a School, College, or University

Select the institution type and prepare the academic workspace for authorized staff.

School / AcademyCollegeUniversity

Symptoms

  • The academic dashboard or expected administration areas are missing.
  • An institution creator or staff member has an unexpected role.

Likely causes

  • School, College, and University have separate academic experiences and role eligibility.
  • Some institution-wide controls are reserved for owners or administrators; University department controls may require Dean / HOD assignment.

Steps to try

  1. 1Choose the institution type during signup: School / Academy, College, or University.
  2. 2Complete institution details from the setup flow and confirm the workspace type with its owner.
  3. 3Have the owner invite staff using the role that matches their responsibilities. Do not assign student or parent roles to staff.
  4. 4Create the academic records required by the institution, starting with departments/courses where those are supported.
  5. 5For a University, have an authorized owner/admin assign the Dean or HOD role and the appropriate department before using department controls.
  6. 6Invite students and parents/guardians only through the institution’s authorized process and use the correct linked records.

Verify the fix

Check that the workspace type is correct, users have eligible roles, and a teacher/faculty member sees only assigned classes or records.

When to contact support

If setup or role eligibility seems incorrect, ask the institution owner first. For a persisted workspace-type error, contact support with the institution name and user role (no student records).
