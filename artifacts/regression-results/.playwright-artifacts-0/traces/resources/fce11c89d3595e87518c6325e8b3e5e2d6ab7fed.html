Choose Solo, Company, School, College, or University

Understand the workspace types and why features differ between them.

All workspaces

Symptoms

  • A tool shown in a guide is not visible in your workspace.
  • You are unsure which workspace type to select.

Likely causes

  • WorkGrind separates personal, business, and education features by persisted workspace type.
  • Some features also depend on role and subscription entitlements.

Steps to try

  1. 1Choose Solo for personal planning and individual work.
  2. 2Choose Company for team operations and business capabilities such as CRM and client collaboration.
  3. 3Choose School / Academy for school-level academic administration.
  4. 4Choose College for higher-education departments, courses, and academic operations.
  5. 5Choose University for institution-wide administration and authorized Dean / HOD department controls.
  6. 6If an existing workspace has the wrong type, ask its owner to contact support before creating another workspace; do not try to change type through a URL or browser tools.

Verify the fix

Compare the workspace type and your assigned role with the page or feature you expect to use.

When to contact support

Contact the workspace owner for ordinary access questions. For an incorrect workspace classification, contact WorkGrind support with the workspace name.
