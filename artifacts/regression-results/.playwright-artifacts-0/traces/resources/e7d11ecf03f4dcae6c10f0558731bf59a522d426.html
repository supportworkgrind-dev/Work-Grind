Use CRM and Client Portal features

Understand where business customer records and client collaboration belong.

Company

Symptoms

  • CRM or Client Portal is missing, or a client cannot access shared material.

Likely causes

  • These are Company-oriented features and are not general-purpose education or Solo modules.
  • Role, plan access, client invitation, or explicit sharing can restrict access.

Steps to try

  1. 1Confirm you are in the correct Company workspace.
  2. 2Ask the workspace owner/admin to verify your role and plan access to the relevant module.
  3. 3For CRM records, confirm you have permission to view the record and that filters are not hiding it.
  4. 4For the Client Portal, share only the intended items through the company’s portal workflow and give the client the correct portal invitation or access link.
  5. 5If access should be revoked, ask an authorized admin to remove the client/member access promptly.

Verify the fix

Check the shared view using an authorized client account and confirm it contains only intended records.

When to contact support

Contact your company admin first. When escalating, provide record identifiers only if appropriate; never attach a client’s confidential information without authorization.
