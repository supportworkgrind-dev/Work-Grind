Troubleshoot exams, results, and fees

Find the right academic record and protect confidential grade and financial information.

School / AcademyCollegeUniversity

Symptoms

  • An exam or result is unavailable, a fee balance appears unexpected, or a payment update is missing.

Likely causes

  • The user may not be authorized for the record, the student link may be wrong, or a payment may still be processing.
  • Financial and academic records are scoped to institution and role.

Steps to try

  1. 1Confirm the student and academic period using the institution’s approved process.
  2. 2Ask an authorized administrator to verify the exam/result record and the user’s relationship to it.
  3. 3For fees, confirm the displayed charge, amount, currency, and payment status with the institution office.
  4. 4If a payment was submitted, retain the provider receipt and allow the institution/payment update to be confirmed before paying again.
  5. 5Do not post grades, student identifiers, or payment details into a public support request.

Verify the fix

Have an authorized institution office confirm the corrected result or fee balance in the proper account.

When to contact support

Contact the institution first for academic or fee disputes. For an application error, send WorkGrind support the page and error text with sensitive student/payment details removed.
