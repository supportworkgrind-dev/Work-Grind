Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in to read Help Center articles?+

No. Help articles and public setup guidance are available without signing in. Application pages require sign-in and may also require an authorized role.

Why do my menus differ from a guide?+

WorkGrind features depend on the persisted workspace type, your role, assignments, and subscription entitlements. Company CRM and education records are not universal modules.

Who can manage billing?+

Billing actions are limited to authorized workspace owners, founders, or admins. Ask one of them to review plan status and limits.

Can support troubleshoot private student or client records?+

Send a redacted description and safe technical details first. Do not send private records through a general contact form unless an authorized support process explicitly requests them.

Still stuck?

Send support the page URL, exact error, browser, and steps to reproduce. Keep passwords, access tokens, payment details, and private records out of the report.

Contact support

The public contact form does not require sign-in. For protected workspace actions, sign in and return to support.
