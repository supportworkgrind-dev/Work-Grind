Contact support with a useful error report

Collect enough diagnostic detail to investigate without exposing secrets or private records.

All workspaces

Symptoms

  • You have followed a relevant guide but the problem remains, or you need to report an application issue.

Likely causes

  • Support may need the affected route, visible error, browser, and steps to reproduce before it can identify the issue.

Steps to try

  1. 1Copy the page URL where the problem occurred.
  2. 2Copy the exact on-screen error message; remove any private record contents.
  3. 3Note your browser and version, device type, approximate time, and workspace type/role if relevant.
  4. 4Write numbered steps that reliably reproduce the issue and state what you expected versus what happened.
  5. 5Open the public Contact page and select Technical Support. Never include passwords, access tokens, verification codes, full payment-card details, or unrelated personal/student/client data.

Verify the fix

After submitting, keep any confirmation message or reference. If the public form reports a delivery error, retry later or provide the same safe details through the available support contact route.

When to contact support

Use the Contact page at /contact. For sign-in problems, explain that you are unable to access the in-app support workflow; the public form does not require an application login.
